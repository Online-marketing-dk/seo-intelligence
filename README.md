# Online Marketing SEO Intelligence

SEO Intelligence is developed by Online Marketing to help website owners identify, analyze and improve important technical and on-page SEO elements directly within WordPress.

Online Marketing was established in Denmark in 1995 and has worked professionally with search engine optimization since 1999.

## SEO Intelligence

SEO Intelligence provides website analysis and optimization tools designed to identify SEO issues and make large websites easier to review and maintain.

The system analyzes important SEO elements such as:

- Page titles
- Meta descriptions
- H1 and H2 headings
- Canonical URLs
- 404 errors
- Redirects
- Internal links
- Schema and structured data
- On-page SEO signals

SEO Intelligence helps identify pages requiring attention and provides tools for reviewing and improving SEO information across multiple pages.

## AI-assisted optimization

SEO Intelligence includes AI-assisted functionality for generating optimization suggestions across multiple pages.

AI-generated suggestions are presented for review and approval before changes are applied.

This allows AI to assist with larger optimization tasks while keeping the website owner or SEO specialist in control of the final changes.
